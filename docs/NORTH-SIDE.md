# Chicago North Side

The North Side extends the same resident Chicago world from Millennium Park through Old Town and Lincoln Park to Wrigleyville. The context supplies mapped streets, neighborhood buildings, park and beach terrain, inland ponds, the Lake Michigan shoreline, Diversey and Belmont harbors, piers, representative boats and the northern continuation of Lake Shore Drive. The authored [Old Town and beach-house landmarks](NORTH-SIDE-LANDMARKS.md), [Lincoln Park Zoo](LINCOLN-PARK-ZOO.md) and [Wrigley Field](WRIGLEY-FIELD.md) use this same coordinate frame and their own detailed geometry.

The origin remains 41.878876° N, 87.635918° W, with metres east/up/south. Street grade is y=0; Lake Michigan and the Chicago River use the existing application datum y=−5.7. South Pond is y=−0.55 and the Alfred Caldwell Lily Pool is y=−0.25; other inland ponds and the South Lagoon are shallow relative to adjacent paths, at y=−0.5. These are authored relative elevations chosen from the photographed relationships, not surveyed heights. Sloping sand replaces the opaque ground beneath the beaches and descends toward the lake; it is a geometric interpretation of the mapped outlines, not a measured beach profile.

The main offline extract covers **41.880–41.962° N, −87.670–−87.607° E**. Supplemental mapped shoreline and road geometry extends beyond the curated northern views. After source-ID deduplication against Chicago, Lakefront and Museum Campus, the derivative contains **31,425 building footprints/parts, 1,361 landscape surfaces, 19,878 path segments, 3,752 mapped tree positions, 709 pier polylines and 78 quay/breakwater polylines**. A separate collection adds **248 authored representative canopy positions** inside mapped woodland and scrub polygons; these are not OSM tree nodes. There are also 46 sand/beach surfaces, 34 separate shallow-water surfaces and 3,961 retained landmark records. These are resource counts, not counts of unique real-world buildings or objects visible at once. Authored-site masks suppress source parents and parts that would otherwise fill the zoo, stadium or existing museum interiors.

Tagged heights supply 112 of the new generic records; 15,719 use mapped levels and 15,594 use explicitly marked estimates. Most untagged residential context uses a bounded low-rise fallback. Near Wells Street, Old Town residential blocks, Clark Street and Wrigleyville, buildings have modeled window frames, sills, cornices, doors, steps, selected storefronts, shallow awnings, projecting signs and planters. More distant facades use fewer modeled bays and stories. Brick patterns, window occupancy, shop treatments and occasional static pedestrians are authored interpretations rather than surveyed businesses or a live population. Finite warm street and shop lights support nighttime views.

Wells Street also receives a bounded ground-floor layer on **82 street-facing frontages from the older map resource**, whose building shells, upper facades and roofs remain intact. Colored joinery, glazing, transoms, awnings, abstract signs and planters replace the otherwise generic ground-floor rhythm. Sparse benches and static pedestrians follow geographic spacing, so short source road segments do not leave the sidewalk empty. These additions contribute **28,970 triangles and 12 lights**. Their geometry remains below 5 m and outside the central 9 m carriageway/camera corridor; cross-street carriageways are also avoided. The signs do not name or assert real businesses.

The northern terrain replaces the old unvisited cap from z=−3500 northward and adds a western strip beside the expanded neighborhood map. The previous resource files are preserved byte-for-byte, and the actual southern land/water surfaces remain unchanged. The new land, water, shallow ponds and sloped beaches are triangulated with their holes intact. Existing park surfaces are clipped to these mapped water and beach boundaries, and the original large ground slabs are trimmed so they cannot cover northern ponds, harbors or the river. Curated zoo boardwalks, the Fisher bridge, Gallagher Way and landmark footprints are rendered by their dedicated builders.

Lincoln Park relation **11610689** extends beyond the older extract's z=−6200 clipping boundary. Its additional northern geometry supplies **194.7 hectares of lawn** after subtracting retained coverage, mapped buildings, circulation, water, beaches, rocky shore, parking and athletic hard surfaces. The older 1,360 landscape surfaces remain unchanged. Sparse canopy follows mapped wood/forest/scrub belts around Diversey and Belmont using a deterministic 12 m lattice and boundary setbacks; open recreation lawns and playing fields receive no generated woodland. Heights, individual placements and canopy shapes are representative interpretations. This bounded addition contributes **25,593 triangles and no lights**.

Two Lake Shore Drive carriageway extensions add **5,833.2 m and 5,812.3 m** of mapped pavement. The same six lane IDs retain every earlier traffic point exactly, in its original order; their northern endpoints now lie beyond z=−11000, outside the curated camera corridors. The renderer uses this extended network for its existing fleet, with no duplicate traffic layer. Away from the previously modeled river crossing, road grades use an interpreted common street level rather than a surveyed civil-engineering profile.

The harbors contain **104 deterministic representative boats**, including 65 with the closer hull, cabin, rail and rigging detail. Thirty-two are powerboats in Diversey Harbor: the harbor operator states that bridge restrictions make it a powerboat-only harbor. Belmont has a representative mixture of motorboats and moored sailboats. Every modeled hull is checked against mapped water, docks and the other boats. Occupancy is deliberately sparse and does not claim to reproduce a current slip assignment or full berth inventory. A subset of detailed boats carries restrained warm cabin lighting. [Chicago Harbors — Diversey Harbor](https://www.chicagoharbors.info/harbors/diversey/), [Belmont Harbor](https://www.chicagoharbors.info/harbors/belmont/).

## Reproduction and validation

The exact queries and raw responses are retained in `scripts/data/chicago-north-side-{osm,shoreline,roads}-2026-09-08.{overpass,json}`. The main and shoreline snapshots are **2026-09-08T15:38:06Z**; the road supplement is **2026-09-08T15:45:11Z**. The main response contains 86,381 elements. Original and derived geometry is **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, licensed under [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The prepared JSON retains attribution, license, timestamp and source hashes, with an additional notice in its resource directory. No network connection or source checkout is required by the packaged app.

With the pinned dependency in `scripts/requirements-map.txt` installed:

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare-north-side-context.py
python3 scripts/validate-north-side-context.py
scripts/validate-north-side-geometry.sh
```

The topology validator checks source hashes, unchanged prior resources, constrained-triangulation coverage and nonoverlap, unchanged southern terrain, separation of land/water/beach surfaces, shallow pond exposure, ordered building roofs, authored landmark/boardwalk exclusions, exact older traffic sequences and boat clearance. It also checks the restored park against the raw parent and mapped exclusions, while keeping authored canopy positions distinct from tagged trees. The CPU geometry fixture builds **9,029,728 triangles, 341 lights and 70 materials** for this context alone, with finite positions, unit normals, noncollapsed triangles and valid material references. Four actual-mesh probes verify that restored lawn uses upward-facing grass above the base terrain. The fixture also covers the Art Institute parent/Modern Wing duplicate-shell regression, the interpreted Wrigley marquee approach and the bounded Wells ground-floor layer. These counts exclude the dedicated zoo, Wrigley, church and beach-house builders and all preceding Chicago geometry.

Evidence: [map validation](validation/v1.7/north-side-map-validation.json), [CPU geometry validation](validation/v1.7/north-side-geometry-validation.json) and [complete CPU log](validation/v1.7/north-side-geometry-validation.txt). These establish data and geometry support only. Full-world camera clearance, final day/night imagery, GPU performance and exported media require the separate integrated release checks.
