# Magnificent Mile landmarks

The Magnificent Mile extension is authored metre-scale architecture in the same Chicago world as Willis Tower and Millennium Park. The shared origin is **41.878876°N, 87.635918°W**; x points east, y up, and z south. `EiffelBuilder.magnificentMile()` adds 875 North Michigan Avenue, Water Tower Place, the historic Chicago Water Tower, and the Michigan Avenue portion of the Chicago Avenue Pumping Station. These models replace their corresponding generic map extrusions.

The skyscrapers and historic structures follow the small mapped street-grid rotation, about one to one-and-a-half degrees from cardinal directions. An [Esri World Imagery aerial export covering the four landmarks](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/export?bbox=-87.627,41.896,-87.620,41.901&bboxSR=4326&imageSR=3857&size=1100,1100&format=png&f=html) was visually reviewed on September 8, 2026: it confirms the Water Tower Place slab on the east/southeast of its podium, Hancock north of it, and the historic tower west of Michigan opposite the pumping station. The mosaic visibly displaces tall rooftops; ground footprints therefore remain aligned to OSM. Imagery acquisition date is unknown.

The historic Water Tower’s height was checked against the [Library of Congress HABS elevation and section, sheet 3](https://www.loc.gov/resource/hhh.il0097.sheet/?sp=3). The drawing was visually inspected during this implementation and explicitly dimensions **182 feet 6 inches overall**, or **55.626 m**, an observation floor at **151 feet 6 inches** (46.1772 m), and the cupola base at **165 feet 6 inches** (50.4444 m). This supersedes the map’s rounded 55.4 m. The [1964 HABS written report](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/il/il0000/il0097/data/il0097data.pdf) reproduces the older 154-foot description, accounting for the conflicting figure in historical summaries. The model anchors its windowed observation stage and dome to these measured levels. The rounded metal cupola rises approximately 12 feet 2 inches above its base, inferred from the drawing rather than presented as an independently verified dimension. The tip remains at the exact overall height; lower ornament elevations remain a photographic reconstruction.

## Modeled architecture

875 North Michigan Avenue uses four tapered façades, five approximately twenty-storey X-bracing panels on each face, horizontal structural bands, corner columns, individually modeled recessed windows, narrow mullions, mechanical louvers, and steel edge strips. Lower brace intersections have plates and small fastener heads. Its roof contains a mechanical enclosure and two antenna assemblies with collars, braces, and red obstruction lights. The west forecourt has a retail pavilion, canopy, trees, benches, entrance hardware, and a modeled address sign. [SOM’s project account](https://www.som.com/projects/875-north-michigan-avenue-formerly-john-hancock-center/) establishes the exterior diagonalized tube system, its approximately twenty-storey bracing panels, and the 100-storey scale. [The building operator’s photography](https://875northmichiganavenue.com/) informed the façade’s metal profiles and ground-level character.

Water Tower Place has a pale gray marble podium, modeled panel joints, shopfront glazing and mullions, a rounded corner entry volume, three upper podium glazing tiers, and an extruded name sign. The complete twelve-storey podium includes the hotel and meeting levels above the mall: it uses the map’s 55 m outer volume and 60 m inset hotel plinth. The tower continues to the 261.9 m roof. The narrow tower stands at the eastern, southern edge of the full podium footprint, with individual flush window bays and continuous stone piers. The [Council on Vertical Urbanism building record](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/water-tower-place/739) supplies the 261.9 m height, 74 floors and 1976 completion. [The Skyscraper Museum’s architectural account](https://old.skyscraper.org/EXHIBITIONS/BIG_BUILDINGS/CONTENT/jumbos/j_16.htm) describes its marble tower and retail base. Contemporary [Streeterville frontage photography](https://www.dreamtown.com/neighborhoods/streeterville) was visually inspected to place the glazing, signage and corner entrance. The [hotel’s official meeting-space plan](https://www.ritzcarlton.com/content/dam/marriott-digital/rz/us-canada/hws/c/chirz/en_us/document/assets/rz-chirz-meeting-space-floor-plan-22620.pdf) identifies its twelfth-floor lobby and meeting level, consistent with [BAMO’s architectural account](https://bamo.com/projects/ritz-carlton-chicago/). The 55/60 m intermediate elevations come from the mapped building parts, not from an assumed height for the seven-to-eight-level shopping mall alone. Tenant-specific storefront graphics and future retail conversion proposals are not reproduced.

The Chicago Water Tower combines its broad square base, two rising central stages, long tapered octagonal shaft, upper lantern and metal roof. The geometry includes corner turrets, stepped ring moldings, parapet merlons, corbels, paired windows, gabled portals, lancet outlines, sill blocks, and shallow stone course relief. The nearby pumping station repeats the limestone, turrets, arched apertures, cresting and steep roof forms. The [Chicago Architecture Center’s historic account and two photographs](https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/chicago-water-tower) supplied the full silhouette and close view of its stone detailing. HABS identifies four entrance stoops and the rusticated yellow-cream Joliet limestone. Small planting beds and benches establish scale around the landmark.

## Night reference and materials

Windows choose stable room occupancy per modeled pane, so lights remain aligned with glazing and do not crawl over the façade during motion. Most rooms stay dark, with a mix of warm and cooler interior colors. The Hancock crown uses a restrained neutral-white presentation and separate red obstruction beacons. The real crown can show event-dependent colors: the lighting manufacturer’s [Acclaim project account](https://www.acclaimlighting.com/blogs/projects/john-hancock-center) documents the LED replacement above the 98th floor. Its nighttime lakefront photograph and close-up of the installed fixtures were actually viewed for the relative darkness of the tower, luminous crown and dense city lights. No particular event’s current lighting program is claimed.

The historic structures have warm, directional ground lights. Eight azimuths of broad overlapping light banks cover all of the Water Tower’s octagonal faces, while low-output fill sources reveal the base and recessed central pavilion. The banks represent the effective spread of multiple terrace projectors; compact visible housings remain on the ledges, and their effective stand-off positions are not an as-built fixture survey. The original corner accents are dimmer to avoid isolated overexposed patches. The modern retail fronts and forecourt use softer canopy lighting. Water Tower Place adds modest display emission and canopy banks on its Michigan Avenue and Pearson Street fronts, while the tall marble podium stays mostly unlit. These are renderer light sources with shadowing, distance falloff and reflected light, alongside visible fixture geometry. The light positions and power are authored photographic approximations rather than a photometric installation survey.

## Navigation, validation and limits

The lakefront tour owns the camera paths: a ground-level historic tower walk, Water Tower Place frontage, and an elevated Hancock orbit. Landmark coordinates in `MagnificentMileLayout` provide stable anchors; integrated route validation checks the camera against the complete context. Roof and brace views are architectural flyovers; these landmarks do not add public interior tours.

Run the bounded CPU fixture with:

```sh
scripts/validate-magnificent-mile.sh
```

It generates only these landmarks and checks finite geometry, material indices, normalized normals, landmark heights, the Water Tower observation/cupola levels, the 55/60 m Water Tower Place podium setbacks, and triangle/light budgets. The refined complete model passed with 219,434 triangles, 54 light sources, no nonfinite vertices or degenerate triangles, and maximum normal-length error below 0.000001. Collinear Hancock mullions use continuous geometry, removing more than 147,000 hidden end-cap triangles. `scripts/validate-playback.sh` supplies separate integrated route and clearance checks in the complete Chicago world. Rendered day/night review is tracked with the release’s gallery and media validation artifacts.

The architectural models are procedural reconstructions. Close details such as window spacing, brackets, joints, signs, benches and roof plant are authored to match the visual vocabulary; they are not fabrication drawings. The former Hancock’s true sunken plaza is represented as a paved forecourt with terraced edges because the current shared street surface does not excavate below grade. The pumping station’s rear operational campus is map-derived context. No downloaded building textures, proprietary BIM models, copied tenant graphics, or photographic images are shipped as model assets.
