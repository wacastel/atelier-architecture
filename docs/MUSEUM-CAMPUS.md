# Museum Campus, Burnham Harbor and McCormick Place

Museum Campus extends the resident Chicago world south from Grant Park. Its mapped context supports the Field Museum, Shedd Aquarium, Adler Planetarium, Soldier Field, Burnham Harbor and the four-building McCormick Place campus. All destinations use the same Willis origin: 41.878876° N, 87.635918° W, metres east/up/south. Street grade is y=0 and the established lake surface is y=−5.7. These are the application's shared relative elevations, not surveyed NAVD88 elevations.

The offline OpenStreetMap extract covers 41.839–41.872° N and −87.638–−87.594° E. The derivative adds 3,382 generic building footprints/parts, 626 landscape surfaces, 5,189 path segments, 2,745 mapped tree positions, 358 pier polylines and 32 quay/breakwater polylines after deduplication against the previous Chicago/Lakefront resources. Counts describe source geometry; some generic buildings and paths are suppressed by authored landmark masks. The 4,213 landmark records also retain museum/convention footprints, holes, tags and triangulation for the authored building models. Seventy-five parent/part IDs and their site masks prevent generic shells from filling the new interiors.

The previous Lakefront resource remains unchanged. Its actual land/water geometry covers Museum Campus and Burnham Harbor. The newly visible southern cap at z=3500–4200 is replaced in the rendered scene using the fresh shoreline, removing the old extract’s artificial land closure at z=3783. Land/water surface boundaries north of z=3500 remain unchanged; museum approach masks remove conflicting generic buildings, paths and trees. A non-overlapping strip then extends the bounded terrain south from z=4200 to z=7800. Two mapped Lake Shore Drive carriageway extensions add 4,117.7 and 3,933.6 metres of pavement, respectively. The same six traffic lane IDs preserve every original northern point in its original order; their remote southern endpoints now lie beyond the curated McCormick views. No new fleet is added on top of the existing one. Road grades away from the established river crossing are an interpreted street level, not a civil-engineering elevation model.

Burnham Harbor includes 151 deterministic representative moored boats. The modeled hulls are tested against actual mapped harbor polygons, every dock and one another. Near the walkthrough, sailboats have furled sails, masts, standing rigging, fenders, rails, teak decks and glazed cabins; motorboats have contrasting hulls and raised cabins. The harbor operator publishes 1,126 slips, so the model deliberately represents a subset of occupancy rather than a current berth inventory. Dock power pedestals illuminate both sides of the piers, and 31 occupied detailed boats carry restrained warm cabin strips with finite 11 m courtesy lights. Waterfront benches, promenade fixtures and mapped tree positions provide scale during daytime and nighttime tours. [Chicago Harbors — Burnham Harbor](https://www.chicagoharbors.info/harbors/burnham/).

Soldier Field combines the retained Doric colonnades and southern horseshoe wall with a contemporary asymmetric seating bowl. Two open stone porticos contain columns, stepped entablatures and restrained ceiling lights. The taller western seating bank, lower eastern glass-and-metal crescent, open northern upper bowl, exposed supports, individual seat backs, aisles, scoreboards and floodlight arrays follow the architectural relationships visible in current aerial photographs. The field uses regulation playing proportions and original abstract markings; there are no downloaded team textures. The stadium is an authored architectural interpretation, including representative seating rather than an exact seat inventory. Its portico corridor and field are navigable. [Chicago Architecture Center — Soldier Field](https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/soldier-field), [Soldier Field — About](https://www.soldierfield.com/stadium-info/about).

Generic context uses tagged heights when present, levels-derived heights otherwise, then explicitly marked estimates. Untagged harbor offices, boathouses, visitor facilities and seasonal stands use a bounded 5.5–8 m low-rise fallback informed by the aerial, rather than the city-block height fallback. Tagged heights are preserved; the raw estimated values remain available in the source derivative. Several landmark height tags conflict with current reference images: the Lakeside Center parent is tagged 30 m while roof parts claim 100 m, and some hotel floor/height tags disagree. Authored buildings resolve those conflicts separately; raw tags remain available for review.

The Museum Campus Cafe (way445721198) also uses an explicitly estimated 4.5 m height, replacing an unsupported 34 m fallback. The unnamed rounded structure (relation18999713, outer766375454) is modeled as the single-storey glazed ticket pavilion visible in the engineer’s reference photograph: thin cream canopy, mullions and restrained interior kiosk lighting. Its 4.5 m canopy height is inferred from the photograph’s door/kiosk scale, not a mapped measurement. Nearby footpath ribbons share mitered joints to avoid triangular gaps at bends.

The separate Hilton Chicago Conference Hall at Grant Park (way147217805) and Donnelly Armory (way210537559) remain generic context and are not mistaken for McCormick's Hilton triplex or historic Lakeside Technology building.

## Reproduction and validation

Raw responses and exact Overpass queries are in `scripts/data/chicago-museum-campus-*-2026-09-08.{json,overpass}`. The main database timestamp is **2026-09-08T12:08:37Z**; the supplemental shoreline and southern-road responses are dated **2026-09-08T12:21:50Z**. Geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The bundled MuseumCampusContext.json retains its attribution, license, license URL and source hashes; each raw response also retains its ODbL notice. Attribution is displayed in the application and exported video captions. The prepared JSON is loaded from the app's resource bundle before any SwiftPM development fallback, so the installed app does not need network access or the source checkout.

With the dependencies in `scripts/requirements-map.txt` installed, run:

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare-museum-campus-context.py
python3 scripts/validate-museum-campus-context.py
scripts/validate-museum-campus-geometry.sh
```

The context validator checks triangulation coverage, land/water disjointness, the southern seam, prior-resource hash preservation, old/new ID deduplication, landmark exclusions, road connectivity, unchanged northern lane sequences and every boat's placement. The CPU mesh fixture builds **763,927 environment triangles plus 614,790 Soldier Field triangles**, with **390 lights** across these two builders. It checks finite positions, unit normals, noncollapsed triangles, valid material references and actual mesh clearance in the portico and field. These counts exclude generic city buildings, other authored museums/convention buildings, and the existing lakefront.

Evidence is saved in [map validation](validation/v1.6/museum-campus-map-validation.json) and [CPU geometry validation](validation/v1.6/museum-campus-geometry-validation.txt). These checks establish source and geometry support; full-world camera clearance, image quality and exported media are validated separately for the integrated release.
