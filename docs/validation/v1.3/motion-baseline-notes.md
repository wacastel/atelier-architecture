The metrics.json and PNG files in this directory are the preserved pre-fix Chicago motion results. They have not been overwritten.

Their stream labeled `raw` was actually the old low-sample preview: presentFragment applied a 3×3 depth/luminance filter with strength 0.62 at four samples. Reconstructed presentation bypassed that legacy filter, and the 128-sample reference also bypassed it. These results correctly exposed a visible reconstruction regression against the previous preview, but they were not an unfiltered Monte Carlo baseline.

The corrected presentation stage performs only exposure, filmic tone mapping, and sRGB encoding at every sample count. A real GPU presentation fixture verifies identical sharp pixel coverage at 1, 4, and 128 samples. New motion reports must be written to a separate directory with the unchanged strict image/temporal improvement thresholds. Their raw-versus-reconstructed percentages are not directly comparable to this directory's legacy baseline.

A coarse night pixel whose center guide sees a dark frame can still legitimately receive jittered samples from an adjacent lit window. The old spatial filter bypassed emitter centers but filtered those neighboring mixed pixels toward darkness, eroding window coverage asymmetrically. The correction preserves current coverage around nearby emitter guides above the existing 0.25-metre night footprint threshold, while retaining spatial noise reduction on smooth distant surfaces away from emitters. A moving mixed-window GPU fixture checks that non-emitter centers keep their legitimate fractional illumination.
