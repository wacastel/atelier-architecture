# Chicago loading-screen facts

The loading screen contains 100 short facts and shows a randomly selected one immediately. Every three seconds it advances through a shuffled bag, showing every fact once before reshuffling and avoiding repeats at bag boundaries. Facts disappear as soon as loading reaches 100%, and are not shown for Paris. Reduce Motion removes movement and transition animation. This is an offline feature: facts and source references ship in the app; no web request runs during loading.

The statements below were checked against institutional, municipal, museum, operator and archival sources on **2026-09-10**. They favor stable historical and architectural facts over schedules, prices, changing attendance totals or world rankings. Wording is original and intentionally brief for a three-second card. Each entry carries its source in the app and links to it here.

## Editorial notes

- The Centennial Wheel height/count come from the Chicago Architecture Center's wheel history; Navy Pier's operator supplies the opening and naming history.
- Cloud Gate's dimensions and fabrication come from Chicago Public Library's archival description of Millennium Park Inc. records.
- The lion facts use the Park District's sculpture record; the two lions are not given invented names or confused poses.
- The Water Tower is not described as the only survivor of the 1871 fire; other buildings survived too.
- Buckingham Fountain's operating status and jet counts are deliberately omitted, since current repairs and differing historical configurations make those unsuitable evergreen loading facts.
- The Chicago Cultural Center's Tiffany dome is not conflated with its separate G.A.R. dome.
- The Wrigley statements use National Park Service landmark documentation instead of unsupported baseball folklore.

## Maintenance and validation

Edit `Sources/ArchitectureEngine/ChicagoFacts.swift`, then update this ledger with the same ID and wording. Every fact must retain a supporting source. Keep the total at 100 and each fact under 180 characters so it remains readable in the compact card. `scripts/validate-chicago-facts.sh` checks the count, IDs, uniqueness, source coverage, display lengths, three-second cadence, shuffled coverage, boundary repeats and delayed/invalid-clock behavior. The UI is separately typechecked with the loading snapshot model.
