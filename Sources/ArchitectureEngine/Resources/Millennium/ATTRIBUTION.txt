MillenniumContext.json is a derived database from the public OpenStreetMap
snapshot timestamped 2026-09-08T00:55:31Z. Source extract and deterministic
conversion are distributed as scripts/data/millennium-osm-2026-09-08.json and
scripts/prepare-millennium-context.py. Coordinates use the existing Willis
Tower origin (41.878876,-87.635918), in metres east / south.

The Cloud Gate, Pavilion, Crown Fountain, Millennium Monument and BP Bridge
surfaces are original reference-based reconstructions. They are not surveyed
scans or official architectural models. See docs/MILLENNIUM.md for references
and model limitations. Reference photography is not bundled or used as textures.
